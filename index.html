About Milestone · Saudi Arabia, 2024

Designed for the modern kitchen. Built around your lifestyle.

Founded in 2024 in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Milestone brings premium kitchen and home appliances together under one brand: modern design, enduring performance, smart technology and quality. Every built-in appliance is planned to sit perfectly with your cabinetry. That is The Integration Art.

  • Consultation & planningSpecialists help you choose each appliance and fit it to your cabinets.
  • Certified installationCertified technicians install every appliance with care.
  • Warranty & after-salesOfficial warranty, maintenance with genuine parts and ongoing support.
  • Kingdom-wide deliveryDelivered safely to every city in Saudi Arabia.
